How to Make Pinterest Pins with AI (Free Prompts)

By Seller Notebook · Updated November 2026

In short

  • A pin is a tall image with a title, a short description and a link to your page.
  • AI can write the pin text from your guide or listing, and make the photo behind the text.
  • We made 52 pins for 9 of our own pages this way. The prompts are below.
  • Check every fact on the pin, and turn on Pinterest's AI-modified label.

Step 1: Set up your account and boards

  • Use a business account. Ours is free. You can turn a personal account into a business account in the settings.
  • Claim your website. Pinterest gives you a short code to add to your site. We did this first, so our pins show our site name.
  • Make a few boards, one per topic. We made three: Etsy SEO tips, AI Prompts for Sellers and Product photo ideas.

Step 2: Write the pin text with AI

Open Gemini, ChatGPT or Claude. Paste your guide or listing, then this prompt:

Prompt: pin ideas from a guide
Here is a guide from my shop's blog:
[paste the guide, or its main points]

Write 5 Pinterest pin ideas for it. Each pin is about a different point from the guide. For each pin give:
- Text on the image: 3 to 7 words
- Pin title: under 100 characters, with the main search phrase near the start
- Description: 1 or 2 sentences that say what the reader will learn
- Board: one of these: [your board names]

Use only facts and numbers from the guide. Don't promise results the guide doesn't show.

Three of the titles we got for our lifestyle photos guide, and posted:

  • 1 Product Photo, 4 Lifestyle Scenes with Free AI
  • Etsy's Rules for AI Product Photos
  • Gemini vs ChatGPT for Product Photos

Check every number. Numbers go out of date. Four of our pins said "20 prompts", and then our prompt pack grew. We hold those pins back until we fix them.

Step 3: Make the pin image

The size: Pinterest recommends a 2:3 ratio, 1000 × 1500 pixels, for its image ads. It says wider images may get cut off in feeds. We used the same size for every pin.

The photo: use your own product photo, or ask AI for one with space for text. Upload your product photo to Gemini or ChatGPT with this prompt:

Prompt: pin photo with space for text
Use the product in this photo. Keep the product exactly the same: same shape, colour, texture and design. Do not change or add anything on the product.

Place it [describe the scene, for example: on a cream linen tablecloth in soft daylight] in the lower third of the image, with the design facing the camera. Leave the top half as calm, empty space for text. Vertical 2:3 format.

Realistic product photo, no text.

Gemini kept the top half empty, as we asked. It also put coffee in the mug, which we didn't ask for. The image came out 848 × 1264 pixels, close to 2:3.

The text: add it in any design tool that saves a 1000 × 1500 image. We added our text ourselves, so every word is exact. Keep it short and big, with your site name at the bottom.

Step 4: Publish the pin

In Pinterest's Pin builder, upload the image and fill in:

  • Title: from Step 2. For ads, Pinterest allows up to 100 characters, and the first 40 may show in feeds.
  • Description: one or two sentences from Step 2.
  • Link: the exact page the pin is about, not just your home page.
  • Board: the board for that topic.
  • Tagged topics: we added "Selling on Etsy" to every pin.
  • Mark as AI-Modified: we turned it on for every pin.

Check the pin one more time, then publish.

Our 52 pins: what we learned

  • One guide, many pins. Our 9 pages gave us 52 pins. Each pin is about one point: a tip, a rule, a number or a before and after.
  • Real photos stand out. Our pins with the mug photos look warmer than the text-only ones.
  • Post a few a day. We post 2 or 3 pins a day, mixed topics, instead of all 52 at once.
  • Results take time. We started posting on 25 November 2026, so it's too early to share numbers. We will add them here.

FAQ

Do I need a Pinterest business account?

We used a free business account. It let us claim our website and see the Pin builder options we describe here.

Should I mark my pins as AI-modified?

Pinterest's Pin builder has a "Mark as AI-Modified" option. We turned it on for every pin, because AI wrote the text and made or edited the photos.

What size should a pin be?

Pinterest recommends a 2:3 ratio, 1000 × 1500 pixels, for its image ads, and says wider images may get cut off in feeds. We used 1000 × 1500 for all our pins.

How many pins should I make for one guide?

We made 4 to 8 pins per guide, each about a different point. Then we posted 2 or 3 pins a day, mixed topics. That's our approach, not a Pinterest rule.
