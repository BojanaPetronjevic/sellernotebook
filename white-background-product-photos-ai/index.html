White Backgrounds for Product Photos with AI

By Seller Notebook · Updated November 2026

In short

  • Free AI tools like Gemini can put your product on a white background in under a minute.
  • Use a clear prompt: change the background, keep the product the same.
  • Always check the result. AI can change small details of your product.
  • For Amazon, check that the background is pure white.

Why a white background?

A white background puts all the attention on your product. It also looks the same across your whole shop.

  • Amazon: the main image needs a pure white background. Amazon means RGB 255, 255, 255. Check the current rules in Seller Central.
  • Etsy and your own store: white is not required. But it looks clean, and it works well as your first photo.

You don't need a studio for this. A normal phone photo and a free AI tool are enough to start.

Step 1: Start with a good photo

AI works better when the photo is good. Before you start:

  • Use soft light. A window during the day works well.
  • Show the whole product. Don't cut off the handle, the top or the bottom.
  • Keep it sharp. Tap on the product on your phone to focus.
  • Remove your location. Phone photos often save where you took them. Turn off location for the camera, or remove it before you share the photo online.

Step 2: Ask the AI

Open Gemini or ChatGPT. Upload your photo and paste this prompt:

Prompt: white background
Edit this photo: remove the background. Keep the product exactly the same: same shape, colour, details and design. Do not change or add anything on the product. Put it on a pure white background (#FFFFFF) with a soft natural shadow under the product. Product photo style.

The words "do not change or add anything" matter. Without them, AI tools often "improve" the product.

Then download the full-size image. In Gemini, use the download button on the image.

Step 3: Check the result

Put the new image next to your original. Look at these things:

  • The product: is every detail the same? Look at the design, colour, texture and any text.
  • The edges: are they clean? Look closely at handles, straps and gaps.
  • The background: is it really white, or light grey in the corners?
  • The shadow: does it look natural? Or does the product float?
  • The size: is the image big enough? Amazon zoom needs large images. Check the current minimum.

If something is wrong, ask the AI to fix that one thing. For example: "Keep everything the same, but make the background pure white in the corners."

What we found in our test

We tested this in November 2026 with the mug above. We used a free Gemini account.

  • Speed: Gemini made the white background in under a minute.
  • The background: we measured the pixels. About 87% were pure white. About 11% were almost white. So it looks white, but it isn't 100% pure white everywhere.
  • The product: the shape, handle and design stayed the same. But the dark speckles in the glaze look stronger, and the cat's eyes look a bit different.
  • The size: the image was 848 × 1264 pixels.

We also tried ChatGPT on its free plan. After a few images, it said we had hit the free image limit and had to wait 24 hours.

In another test, we asked ChatGPT to edit a plain mug. It changed the smooth glaze into a speckled one, without being asked. That is why checking matters.

remove.bg was a popular free background remover. Its website now says it is moving to Canva, and that the standalone site closes on 1 December 2026.

One rule: change the background, not the product

AI can also add designs, change colours or create a whole scene. That can be fun for social media. But in a listing, your photo must show the real product the buyer will get.

If the photo and the product don't match, buyers get disappointed. That can mean returns and bad reviews.

FAQ

Can I do this for free?

Yes. We made the white background with a free Gemini account. Free plans have limits: ChatGPT's free plan stopped us after a few images and asked us to wait 24 hours.

Is the AI background white enough for Amazon?

Not always. Amazon asks for a pure white main image background (RGB 255, 255, 255). In our test, most of the background was pure white, but some parts were almost white. Check your image, and check Amazon's current image rules in Seller Central.

Can AI add a design or change my product?

It can, but you shouldn't use that in a listing. Your photo must show the real product the buyer will get. Use AI to change the background, not the product.

What about remove.bg?

remove.bg says its background removal is moving to Canva. Its own website says the standalone site closes on 1 December 2026.
