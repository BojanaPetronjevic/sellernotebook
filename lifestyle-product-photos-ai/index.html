Lifestyle Product Photos with Free AI (Tested)

What a lifestyle photo is for

A photo on white shows exactly what the product looks like. A lifestyle photo shows how it fits into a buyer's life: a mug on a breakfast table, a candle on a shelf, a bag on someone's shoulder.

It helps buyers picture the size and the mood. Use it next to your clear product photos, not instead of them. For the white photo, see White Backgrounds for Product Photos with Free AI.

Step 1: Start with a clear photo

The AI copies your product from the photo you upload. The clearer the photo, the closer the copy.

  • Show the whole product, with the design facing the camera.
  • Use soft daylight. Dark photos lose detail.
  • One product per photo. More items confuse the AI.

Step 2: Ask for one scene at a time

Open Gemini or ChatGPT. Upload your photo and paste this prompt. Change the scene line to the one you want.

Prompt: lifestyle scene
Use the product in this photo. Keep the product exactly the same: same shape, colour, texture and design. Do not change or add anything on the product.

Place it [describe the scene, for example: on a wooden breakfast table by a window in soft morning light, next to a plate with a croissant].

Realistic product photo, natural shadows, no text.

The four scenes we used, ready to paste into the line above:

  • Breakfast: on a wooden breakfast table by a window in soft morning light, next to a plate with a croissant
  • Desk: on a tidy home office desk next to an open notebook and a pen, in bright daylight
  • Cozy: on a knitted blanket next to a lit candle and a few autumn leaves, in warm evening light
  • Gift: next to an open gift box with tissue paper and a ribbon, on a light wooden table

For the next scene in the same chat, start with "Now a new photo with the same product from my first photo." That keeps the AI working from your real photo, not from its last picture.

Step 3: Compare it with your product

Put the result next to your real item. Check:

  • The design: same size, same place, same details. Zoom in.
  • The shape and colour: no new handle, no new glaze, no brighter colour.
  • The size: does the product look bigger or smaller than it is, next to the other objects?
  • Added things: props are fine, but nothing that a buyer might think is included.

Is one detail wrong? Ask for only that fix: "Keep everything the same, but make the design on the product exactly like in my first photo."

Our test: Gemini vs ChatGPT

We tested this in November 2026 with free accounts. We uploaded the same mug photo to both tools and used the same four prompts.

Gemini: all four scenes

  • The mug stayed close to the original in all four: the shape, the speckled glaze, the cat peeking over the edge, the moon and the stars.
  • Fast: about 10 seconds per scene.
  • It added things we didn't ask for: a drink in the mug every time, and a laptop and mouse on the desk.

ChatGPT: two scenes, then a limit

  • More styled scenes: warm sun, flowers, candles. They look like social media photos.
  • The design changed: the cat came out bigger and drawn a little differently. On a real listing, that would show buyers a product they won't get.
  • Slower: about a minute per scene.
  • A limit on the free plan: at the third scene, ChatGPT said image generation was slower than usual. It offered to make the image later, or to upgrade.

Our pick for product photos: Gemini, because it kept the product the same. Your result may differ with other products, so test both.

Etsy's rules for AI photos

Etsy updated its photo rules in 2026. From the Etsy Help Center and the Seller Handbook:

  • Keep one unedited photo. Most listings need at least one unedited photo of the actual item against a real background. It doesn't have to be the first photo.
  • AI is allowed in your other photos, for example to change the background, as long as the item doesn't look different.
  • Don't change the item. No new colour, texture or design, and no hidden damage.
  • Say that you used AI. If you used AI to edit listing photos, Etsy asks you to note it in the listing description.
  • Make the first photo the clearest one. It's the first thing buyers see.

Selling on Amazon, eBay or your own store? Check their current photo rules before you upload.

FAQ

Can I use only AI lifestyle photos in my Etsy listing?

No, not for most items. Etsy requires at least one unedited photo of the actual item against a real background. Your other photos can be edited with AI, as long as they show the item as it really is.

Do I have to say that my photos were made with AI?

On Etsy, yes. Etsy asks sellers who use AI to edit listing photos to say so in the listing description.

Which is better for lifestyle photos, Gemini or ChatGPT?

In our test, Gemini kept the mug closer to the original. ChatGPT made brighter, more styled scenes, but it redrew the design on the mug. Try both with your own product, and pick the one that keeps it the same.

Is it free?

We used free accounts. Gemini made all four scenes. ChatGPT made two, then asked us to wait or upgrade. Free limits change often.
