How to Make Mockups with Free AI (Tested)

By Seller Notebook · Updated November 2026

In short

  • A mockup shows your design in a real place: a print in a frame on a wall, on a shelf, in someone's hands.
  • Free Gemini and ChatGPT can make one from your design file in under a minute. You don't need a camera.
  • The key rule: change the room, not the design. Zoom in and check every shape and letter.
  • On Etsy, a printable also needs a preview of the actual design, not only mockups.

What a mockup is for

If you sell printables, wall art or designs for print-on-demand, you often don't have a physical item to photograph. A mockup fills that gap. It shows buyers how the design will look in a home: the size next to a sofa, the colours in a room, the mood.

Mockups work best next to a clear image of the design itself. Buyers want to see both: the exact file they get, and how it looks on a wall.

Step 1: Start with your design file

The AI copies your design from the file you upload. Give it a clean one.

  • Upload the flat design, not a photo of a screen.
  • Use the same shape as the print. A tall print should be a tall file, so the AI keeps the proportions.
  • One design per upload. For a set of prints, make each mockup separately.

Step 2: Ask for one scene at a time

Open Gemini or ChatGPT. Upload your design and paste this prompt. Change the scene line to the one you want.

Prompt: framed print mockup
Make a realistic mockup photo with this print.

Put the print [describe the frame and the scene, for example: in a thin light oak frame with a white mat, hanging on a warm white wall above a beige sofa in a bright living room].

Keep the artwork exactly the same: same text, letters, shapes, colours and position. Don't add, remove or change anything in the artwork. Portrait format.

The scenes we used, ready to paste into the line above:

  • Living room: in a thin light oak frame with a white mat, hanging on a warm white wall above a beige sofa in a bright living room
  • Shelf: in a thin black frame, leaning against the wall on a wooden shelf, next to a small vase with dried flowers and two books, in soft daylight
  • Nursery: in a white frame on the wall of a calm nursery, above a wooden crib with a soft blanket, in pastel light
  • Desk: in a thin black frame, leaning against the wall on a tidy home office desk next to a laptop and a cup of coffee, in soft daylight
  • In hands: no frame, as a paper sheet held in two hands above a wooden table, close-up

For the next scene in the same chat, start with "Now a new mockup with the same print."

Step 3: Check every detail

Open the mockup next to your design and zoom in. Check:

  • The shapes: same size, same place. Look at the edges and the small details.
  • The text: read every word, letter by letter.
  • The colours: room light can make them look warmer. A little is normal. A new colour is not.
  • The proportions: is the design stretched, squeezed or cut off?
  • The props: look for brand names or logos the AI added, for example on a laptop. Ask the AI to remove them, or crop or blur them.

Is one detail wrong? Ask for only that fix: "Keep everything the same, but make the artwork exactly like the print I uploaded."

Our test: Gemini vs ChatGPT

We tested this in November 2026 with free accounts. We made two simple prints ourselves: an abstract one with shapes, and a text print, because AI often gets letters wrong.

Gemini: six mockups

  • The designs stayed close to the original: the same shapes and colours, and every letter of the text print.
  • Small changes when you zoom in: in the nursery, the arches came out narrower. In the hands, the design filled more of the paper, and the paper was whiter.
  • It added a brand name: the laptop on the desk had a laptop brand's name on it. We blurred it.
  • Fast: under 20 seconds per mockup.
  • Calm, simple rooms that keep the focus on the print.

ChatGPT: two mockups

  • The designs stayed the same: the shapes, the colours and every letter.
  • More styled scenes: flowers, a candle, sunlight and shadows on the wall. They look like social media photos.
  • Slower: about 30 to 40 seconds per mockup.
  • No limit message on the free plan for two images. In an earlier test, ChatGPT slowed down at the third image.

Our pick: both worked well for mockups. Use Gemini for quick, plain mockups, and ChatGPT when you want a styled photo for social media. Your design may give different results, so check every one.

Etsy's rules for mockups

From the Etsy Help Center, checked in November 2026:

  • Printables and templates: include a preview of the actual content buyers will receive. Show the design itself, not only mockups.
  • Print-on-demand: if a production partner prints your design on a standard product, like a t-shirt or a mug, you may use a stock mockup photo if its owner allows it.
  • All listing images must show what buyers will really get.
  • AI-made art: Etsy allows it when you play a clear creative role, and you must say that you used AI.

Selling somewhere else? Check that platform's current image rules before you upload.

FAQ

Can I sell printables on Etsy with only AI mockups?

No. For digital downloads with a visual part, like a printable, Etsy asks you to include a preview of the actual content buyers will receive. Add a clear image of the design itself next to your mockups.

Can AI keep the text on my print correct?

In our test, yes: Gemini and ChatGPT both kept every letter of our short text print. Longer text or small letters may come out wrong, so zoom in and read every word.

Which is better for mockups, Gemini or ChatGPT?

Both kept our designs. Gemini was faster and made plain, calm rooms. ChatGPT made bigger, more styled images with flowers and sunlight. Try both with your own design.

Is it free?

We used free accounts. Gemini made six mockups and ChatGPT made two, with no limit message. Free limits change often.
