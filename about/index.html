About Seller Notebook

Seller Notebook is a small, independent site. It has practical AI guides for people who run an online shop on their own.

Why this site exists

You have something to sell. But you don't always have time to make it stand out. Many sellers already use AI to write text and make images.

The problem: most advice about AI comes from companies that sell AI tools. Each one recommends its own product. The rest is spread across forums.

Seller Notebook fills that gap. You get step-by-step guides, prompts to copy and honest notes on tools. They take you from a plain idea to a page ready to publish.

Who writes it

Seller Notebook is written by an SEO and content specialist. That job is all about how people search, and what makes them click and buy. Every guide is written by hand. Every prompt and tool is tested before we recommend it.

How guides are written

  • Facts about platforms and tools are checked on official pages. If we can't confirm something, we say so.
  • Prompts are tried on real products first.
  • Guides are updated when platforms or tools change.

Money

The site may earn small commissions from some links in the future. That never decides what we recommend. Read the affiliate disclosure.
