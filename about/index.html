About Seller Notebook

Seller Notebook is a small, independent site with practical AI guides for people who run an online shop on their own.

Why this site exists

You have something to offer or sell, but not always the time for the creative thinking it takes to present it so that buyers and readers notice. Many sellers already turn to AI to write their text and create their images.

The trouble is that most advice about AI for online sellers is written by companies that sell AI tools, so each one recommends its own product, and the rest is spread across forum threads. Seller Notebook is the in-between: step-by-step guides that take you from a plain, uninspired idea to finished text and a page ready to publish, with prompts you can copy and honest notes on which tools actually help.

Who writes it

Seller Notebook is written by an SEO and content specialist: someone who spends their working days thinking about how people search and what makes them click and buy. Every guide is written by hand, and every prompt and tool is tested before it is recommended.

How guides are written

  • Facts about platforms and tools are checked against official sources. When something can't be confirmed, the guide says so instead of guessing.
  • Prompts are tried on real product examples before they are published.
  • Guides are updated when platforms or tools change.

Money

The site may earn small commissions from some links in the future. That never decides what is recommended. Read the affiliate disclosure.
