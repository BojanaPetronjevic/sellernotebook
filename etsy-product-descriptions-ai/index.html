How to Write Etsy Product Descriptions with AI (Prompts + Examples)

By Seller Notebook · Updated November 2026

In short

  • AI writes good descriptions only from facts you give it. Start with a short fact sheet.
  • Give it a sample of your own writing so it sounds like your shop.
  • Put the most important information first: buyers skim.
  • Check every fact before you publish. AI sometimes invents details.

What AI does well, and where it goes wrong

AI is good at turning a list of facts into smooth, readable text, at offering several versions to choose from and at fixing a description that rambles. It saves the most time when you have many products to list.

It goes wrong in three predictable ways:

  • It invents details it doesn't know, such as materials, sizes or how the item was made.
  • It sounds generic, with phrases like "elevate your space" that could describe any product.
  • It stuffs keywords if you ask for SEO without limits, which makes the text hard to read.

The method below avoids all three.

Step 1: Write a product fact sheet

Before you open any AI tool, write down the facts in a simple list. This is the most important step, because the AI can only be as accurate as what you give it.

  • What the product is and who it is for
  • Materials, size, weight, colours and variations
  • How it is made (handmade, designed by you, printed on demand)
  • Care instructions
  • Personalisation options, if any
  • What is included in the package
  • Anything buyers often ask you

Leave out shipping times and prices: those belong in your listing settings and shop policies, and it is easy to forget to update them in the description.

Step 2: Ask for a first draft

Paste your fact sheet into this prompt. The rules at the end stop the AI from inventing details or overselling.

Prompt: first draft
You are helping me write a product description for my Etsy shop.

Product facts:
[paste your fact sheet here]

Write a product description that:
- starts with 1-2 sentences saying what the product is and why someone would want it
- then lists the key details (materials, size, care, what's included) as short bullet points
- ends with one sentence about personalisation or gifting, only if it is in the facts
- uses simple, friendly language
- is between 120 and 200 words

Rules:
- Use only the facts above. Do not invent materials, sizes, origins or features.
- If something important is missing, list it as a question at the end instead of guessing.
- No exaggerated claims like "best", "perfect" or "life-changing".

If the AI asks questions at the end, answer them and ask it to update the description. That is a good sign: it means it isn't guessing.

Step 3: Make it sound like your shop

Generic text is the most common problem with AI descriptions. The fix is to show the AI how you write. Copy two or three paragraphs you wrote yourself, for example from your shop's About section or a listing you're proud of.

Prompt: match my voice
Here is a sample of how I write for my shop:

[paste 2-3 paragraphs you wrote yourself]

Rewrite the product description below so it matches my tone, sentence length and word choice. Keep every fact exactly the same and don't add new ones.

[paste the draft from step 2]

Step 4: Add search words naturally

Buyers search with simple words: what the item is, what it's made of, who it's for and what occasion it suits. Write down 4–6 phrases a buyer might type, then ask the AI to use them where they fit, without forcing them in.

Prompt: add search words
Here are phrases buyers might search for:
[list 4-6 phrases]

Use these phrases in the description below only where they read naturally. Use each one at most once. Put the most important phrase in the first sentence if it fits. Don't change any facts.

[paste your description]
Your listing title and tags matter more for Etsy search than the description. Use the description to answer buyers' questions and help them decide.

Example: before and after

The product below is made up for this guide, to show the method.

Fact sheet: hand-thrown ceramic mug, holds about 350 ml, speckled white glaze, made in small batches so each one looks slightly different, dishwasher safe, gift box included.

BeforeAfter (AI draft from the fact sheet, then edited)
Beautiful handmade mug. Perfect gift. Great for coffee lovers!! A hand-thrown ceramic mug with a soft speckled white glaze, made for slow morning coffee or tea. Because each mug is shaped by hand in small batches, yours will have its own small differences in shape and speckles.

• Holds about 350 ml
• Speckled white glaze
• Dishwasher safe
• Arrives in a gift box

A thoughtful gift for someone who likes handmade things on their kitchen shelf.

The "after" version answers the questions buyers have (size, care, what arrives) and explains why each mug looks a bit different, which prevents disappointed messages later.

Step 5: Check before you publish

Read the final text once, slowly, with your fact sheet next to it:

  • Every fact is true: size, materials, care, what's included.
  • No promises you can't keep: delivery dates, health or safety claims, "lasts forever".
  • It follows Etsy's current rules for how the item is made and described. Check the official seller policies, because they change.
  • It sounds like you when you read it out loud.

FAQ

Is it allowed to use AI for Etsy descriptions?

Using a writing assistant for your own listing text is a common practice, but Etsy's rules can change. What always matters is that the description is accurate and follows Etsy's current seller policies, including its rules about how items are made. Check Etsy's official seller policies before you publish.

Which AI tool should I use?

Any general AI chat assistant (for example ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini) can do this. The free versions are enough to start. The facts you give it matter more than the tool.

Will AI make my listings rank higher in Etsy search?

Not by itself. AI helps you write faster and more clearly. Ranking depends on many things, such as how well your title and tags match what buyers search for, your photos and how buyers react to the listing.

Should I use the same description for similar products?

No. Ask the AI for a separate description for each product, based on that product's own facts. Repeated text makes listings harder to tell apart for buyers.
