How to Choose Etsy Tags with AI (Free Prompt + Examples)

By Seller Notebook · Updated November 2026

In short

  • You get 13 tags of up to 20 characters each. Use all 13.
  • Use short phrases, not single words: "black cat mug", not "black", "cat", "mug".
  • Your category and attributes already count as tags. Don't repeat them on their own.
  • AI is quick at ideas. But check every tag yourself: AI adds details that aren't true.

What tags do, and Etsy's rules

When a buyer searches on Etsy, Etsy looks for listings with matching words. It checks your title, tags, category, attributes and description. Each tag is one more chance to match a search.

The rules, from Etsy's Help Center:

  • Up to 13 tags per listing.
  • Up to 20 characters per tag, spaces included.
  • Letters, numbers and spaces only. Apostrophes and hyphens are fine inside a tag, but not at the start.

You add tags in Shop Manager: Listings, open the listing, then Tags.

What not to put in tags

Etsy's Seller Handbook lists a few things that waste tags:

  • Repeats. "Octopus art" and "octopus print" are almost the same. Pick different phrases.
  • Your category or attribute on its own. They already count as tags.
  • Misspellings. Etsy fixes common typos for the buyer.
  • Plurals. "Diary" also matches "diaries".
  • Other languages. Use your shop's language. Etsy translates.
  • Anything that isn't true. Don't tag "handmade" or "personalized" if it isn't.

Step 1: Fill in category and attributes first

Etsy says categories and attributes "act like tags". So fill them in before you write tags.

  • Category: pick the most specific one. A mug goes in Home & Living › Kitchen & Dining › Drink & Bar Accessories › Drinkware › Mugs.
  • Attributes: add every one that fits, such as colour and material.

Now you don't need "mugs", "white" or "ceramic" as tags on their own. You can still use them inside a longer phrase, like "white ceramic mug".

Step 2: Find the phrases buyers type

Type your item into the Etsy search bar and read the suggestions. Don't press Enter. We show this step by step in how to find real keywords for free.

Then cross out every phrase that isn't true for your product.

Etsy also suggests longer, more exact phrases. "Canvas tote bag" has fewer searches than "tote bag". But the buyer knows what they want. Etsy says these searches tend to lead to more sales.

Is your best phrase longer than 20 characters? Split it into shorter phrases. Etsy's example: "minimalist diamond engagement ring" becomes "minimalist jewellery", "diamond ring" and "engagement ring".

Step 3: Use different kinds of tags

Thirteen tags that all say "cat mug" match only one kind of search. Etsy suggests mixing these kinds:

KindAsk yourselfEtsy's examples
What it isWhat would a buyer call it?striped ceramic mug, set of four coasters
Material or techniqueWhat is it made of? How?hammered cuff, reclaimed wood frame
Who it's forWho would buy it, or get it as a gift?teacher gift, gifts for new moms
OccasionWhen would someone buy it?first anniversary, stocking stuffers
Problem it solvesWhat does it help with?wardrobe organisation, indoor garden
StyleWhat look is it?art deco lamp, minimalist ring
SizeDoes size matter for this item?large beach bag, tiny gold hoops

Occasion tags are fine for items that suit an occasion, even if they weren't made for it. Etsy's occasion attribute is only for items made for that occasion.

Step 4: Ask AI for 13 tags

Paste your facts, category, attributes and the phrases you kept into this prompt. It works in ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini.

Prompt: 13 Etsy tags
Write 13 Etsy tags for my listing.

Product facts:
[paste your facts]

Category: [your Etsy category]
Attributes I already added: [for example colour, material]

Phrases buyers search for (all true for this product):
[paste the phrases you kept]

Rules:
- Each tag is 20 characters or fewer, counting spaces.
- Use phrases of 2-3 words, not single words.
- Use only letters, numbers and spaces.
- Don't add a tag that only repeats my category or an attribute.
- Make every tag different. No two tags that mean the same thing.
- Use only the facts above. Don't invent size, features or uses.
- Mix these kinds: what it is, material, style, who it's for, occasion.
- Write the number of characters after each tag.

Asking for the character count saves time. You can see at once if a tag is too long.

Step 5: Check every tag

Read the list once, with your facts next to it:

  • 20 characters or fewer. Count again. That's Etsy's limit.
  • True. Look for words you didn't give the AI, like "coffee", "large" or "handmade".
  • Different. If two tags say almost the same thing, replace one.
  • Not just your category or an attribute. "Mugs" or "ceramic" alone is a wasted tag.
  • Phrases, not single words.
  • All 13 used.

Example: a black cat mug

We ran this prompt on an example product in November 2026. The AI answer was made with Claude and is shown exactly as it came out.

  • Product facts: white ceramic mug, black cat design, cute style, a gift for cat owners. We don't know its size.
  • Category: Mugs.
  • Attributes: colour white and black, material ceramic.
  • Phrases we kept from the Etsy and Amazon search bars: black cat mug, cat mug, ceramic, cute, gift for cat lovers. We crossed out "handmade" and "personalized", because they aren't true.

cat mom gift (12)Keep. Not in our facts, but a fair "who it's for" tag.

All 13 tags were under 20 characters, and the counts were right. The AI followed the length rule well. It broke the "only the facts" rule twice, and the "all different" rule twice.

The final 13 tags, edited by hand

We kept 9 and added 4 of our own:

  1. black cat mug
  2. cute cat mug
  3. kitty mug
  4. black cat cup
  5. white ceramic mug
  6. gift for cat lovers
  7. cat owner gift
  8. cat mom gift
  9. cat dad gift
  10. cat design mug (new: says what's on it)
  11. cute ceramic cup (new: style, material and "cup" together)
  12. cat lover birthday (new: an occasion it suits)
  13. halloween cat mug (new: an occasion a black cat suits)

In our titles guide we left "halloween" out of the title. The mug wasn't made for Halloween, so it shouldn't be in the name. As a tag it's fine, because Etsy allows occasion tags for items that suit an occasion.

Follow-up prompts

Is one tag too long, or are two too similar? Ask the AI to fix only that:

Prompt: fix my tags
Here are my Etsy tags:
[paste your tags]

My product facts:
[paste your facts]

Please:
- shorten any tag over 20 characters, without losing its meaning
- find tags that mean almost the same thing, and suggest a different one for each
- point out any word that isn't in my facts

Show the number of characters after each tag. Don't add new information.

Other short follow-ups that work well:

  • "Give me 5 more 'who it's for' tags, using only my facts."
  • "Which of these tags only repeat my category or attributes?"
  • "This listing gets few visits. Suggest 3 new tags to try instead of tags 4, 7 and 11."

FAQ

How many tags can I use on Etsy?

Up to 13 tags per listing. Etsy recommends using all 13, because each tag is one more chance to match a search.

How long can an Etsy tag be?

Up to 20 characters, spaces included. Tags can use letters, numbers and spaces. Apostrophes and hyphens are allowed inside a tag, but a tag can't start with them.

Should my tags repeat my title?

Your main phrase can be in both. Etsy looks at the title, tags, category, attributes and description together. Use most of your tags for phrases that are not in the title yet, so you match more searches.

Do plurals and misspellings matter?

No. Etsy says it matches root words, so "diary" also matches "diaries". It also corrects common typos, so you don't need misspelled tags.

Can I add tags in other languages?

Etsy says no. Write tags in your shop's language. Etsy translates them when it matches searches. You can add your own translations in the listing's translation settings.

How often should I change my tags?

There's no fixed rule. Etsy suggests checking your Shop Stats and refreshing the tags on listings that get few visits. Change a few tags at a time, so you can see what helped.
