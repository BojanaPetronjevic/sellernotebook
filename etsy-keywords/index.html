How to Find Etsy Keywords for Free

By Seller Notebook · Updated November 2026

In short

  • A keyword is the words a buyer types into Etsy search.
  • Three free places to find them: the Etsy search bar, Google's search suggestions and your own Shop Stats.
  • Cross out every phrase that isn't true for your product, or names another shop or brand.
  • Use AI to sort your list, not to make up keywords. It can't see what buyers type.

What a keyword is on Etsy

A keyword is a word or phrase a buyer types into Etsy search, like "black cat mug". When someone searches, Etsy looks for listings with matching words.

Etsy says "keyword relevance" is one of the things that decide where your listing shows. The others include the quality of your listing, your shop and your customer service. Source: Etsy Help Center.

So good keywords help buyers find you. They can't do all the work alone.

Free place 1: the Etsy search bar

This is the best place to start, because the suggestions come from Etsy itself.

  1. Go to Etsy.
  2. Type the simplest name for your item, like "cat mug". Don't press Enter.
  3. Write down the suggestions that drop down.
  4. Try a more exact version, like "black cat mug", and write those down too.

Suggestions change over time and can differ by country and by person. So check them yourself for each product.

Free place 2: Google's search suggestions

Do the same in Google. Type your item and read the suggestions before you press Enter. Google shows what people search on the whole web, not just on Etsy. So you will see more phrases, and more phrases to cross out.

Google often suggests store names, like "starbucks" or "target". Never use another shop's or brand's name in your listing. It isn't true, and it can break Etsy's rules.

Free place 3: your Shop Stats

Already selling? Your Shop Stats show the search terms people used to find your listings. Etsy confirms this in its Shop Stats glossary. Open Shop Manager, then Stats.

These are the most useful keywords you can get, because real buyers used them to reach you. Look for:

  • Terms that bring visits, but aren't in your title or tags yet.
  • Terms that aren't true for the listing. Buyers who came for those leave without buying.

New shop? You won't have this data yet. Use the first two places, and come back to Shop Stats once you get visits.

Sort your list with AI

Now you have a messy list. AI is quick at sorting it. Paste your product facts and your list into this prompt. It works in ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini.

Prompt: sort my keyword list
I sell on Etsy. Sort this keyword list for one listing.

Product facts:
[paste your facts]

Keywords I collected from the search bars:
[paste your list]

Rules:
- Use only my list. Don't add new keywords.
- Remove phrases with a shop or brand name.
- Remove phrases that aren't true for my product, based on my facts. Say why for each one.
- Remove phrases for a different product.
- Then sort the rest into three groups:
  1. Title: the 1-2 phrases that describe my product best
  2. Tags: phrases for tags (20 characters or fewer)
  3. Description: phrases that fit better in a sentence

"Use only my list" is the most important rule. Without it, AI adds phrases that sound good but that nobody may search for.

Example: a black cat mug

We tried this in November 2026 with the same example product as in our other guides.

  • Product facts: one white ceramic mug, black cat design, cute style, a gift for cat owners. We don't know its size, and it has no lid.
  • Keywords we collected: from Google's suggestions for "black cat mug", "cat mug", "cat mug gift" and "cute cat mug" (US, 18 November 2026), plus "gift for cat lovers" from the Etsy search bar.

The raw list had 19 phrases: black cat mug, black cat mug starbucks, black cat mug anthropologie, black cat mug target, black cat mug amazon, black cat mug dollar tree, black cat mug with lid, cat mug, cat mug bed, cat mugs near me, cat mug gift, cat mug gift set, cute cat mugs, cute cat mug with lid, cute cat mug set, cute cat mug warmer, cute cat mug design, cute cat mug etsy, gift for cat lovers.

Where to use your keywords

Etsy looks at your title, tags, category, attributes and description together. Each has its own guide:

  • Title: your main phrase first. See How to Write Product Titles with AI.
  • Tags: 13 different phrases. See How to Choose Etsy Tags with AI.
  • Description: phrases that fit in a sentence. See How to Write Etsy Descriptions with AI.
  • Attributes: fill in every one that fits, like colour and material. Etsy counts them too.

After you change a listing

Etsy's Help Center gives a few tips:

  • Save your old version first. Etsy suggests downloading your listings as a CSV file before you change titles.
  • Keep the old keywords. If you take a keyword out of the title, move it into your tags, attributes or description.
  • Wait a day. Changes can take up to one day to show in search.

Then check your Shop Stats again after a few weeks. Change a few things at a time, so you can see what helped.

FAQ

Is there a free Etsy keyword tool?

The Etsy search bar and your own Shop Stats are free, and they come from Etsy itself. Other keyword tools guess how many people search. We haven't tested them yet, so we don't recommend one.

Can I see how many people search for a keyword on Etsy?

Not with the free methods in this guide. The search bar shows what people type, but not how often. Shop Stats shows the search terms that brought visitors to your own listings.

Can AI find Etsy keywords for me?

AI can't see what buyers type on Etsy. Use it to sort a list you collected yourself. Don't use it to make up keywords.

Where do I put my keywords?

In your title, tags, attributes and description. Etsy looks at all of them when it matches a search. Use each phrase only where it is true for your product.

How long until changes show in search?

Etsy says changes to a listing can take up to one day to show in search.
