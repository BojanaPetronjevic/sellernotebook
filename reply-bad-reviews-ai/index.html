How to Answer Bad Reviews Politely with AI

By Seller Notebook · Updated November 2026

In short

  • Message the buyer privately first. Fix the problem if you can.
  • On Etsy you can reply publicly only once, and you can't edit the reply. After you reply, the buyer can't edit the review.
  • AI writes a calm reply in seconds. Give it only the real facts, and check every sentence.
  • No tracking numbers, links or personal details in a public reply.

Step 1: Message the buyer first

A bad review is often a problem you can still fix. Take a breath, then send the buyer a private message. Remove names and order numbers before you paste anything into AI.

Prompt: private message after a bad review
A buyer left this review for my [Etsy / online] shop:
[paste the review, without names or order numbers]

What really happened: [the facts: when you shipped, what you already did]
What I can offer: [replacement / refund / return / nothing]

Write a short private message to the buyer. Say sorry for the problem without arguing, and offer what I can do. Don't ask them to change or remove the review. Friendly and calm, 3 to 5 sentences.

Etsy says a quick, helpful answer may lead a buyer to edit their review. Don't ask for it. Just fix the problem.

Step 2: Know Etsy's rules before you reply

From the Etsy Help Center, checked in November 2026:

  • One reply per review. You can't edit it. You can delete it, but you can't post a new one.
  • Your reply locks the review. Once you reply, the buyer can't edit their review or rating, even if you delete your reply. So fix the problem first.
  • 100 days: you can reply up to 100 days after the buyer last edited the review.
  • It's public, and the buyer can't answer it.
  • No tracking numbers, links or private information. Replies that break Etsy's review policies are removed.

Selling on Shopify? Replies depend on the review app you use. Check its settings.

Step 3: Write the public reply

Your public reply is for future buyers as much as for this one. Short, calm and factual works best.

Prompt: public reply to a bad review
A buyer left this review for my [Etsy / online] shop:
[paste the review, without names or order numbers]

What really happened: [the facts]
What I can offer: [replacement / refund / return / nothing]

Write a short public reply, 2 to 4 sentences. Thank them, say sorry for the problem, and say what I can do next. Use only the facts I gave. Don't blame the buyer, the delivery company or the platform. No links, no tracking numbers, no personal details. Calm and friendly.

Our test: 3 reviews, 3 replies

We wrote three example reviews, like the ones small shops often get, for our cat mug. Then we ran the public reply prompt in Claude in November 2026. These are the replies as they came out.

Review (★★☆☆☆): "The mug arrived with a chip on the handle. Disappointed because it was a gift."

Our facts: Shipped in bubble wrap. I can send a replacement or a full refund.

AI reply: Thank you for your review, and I'm so sorry the mug arrived with a chipped handle, especially as it was a gift. I'd like to make it right with a replacement or a full refund. Please send me a message and I'll sort it out straight away.

Review (★★★☆☆): "Cute design but it's smaller than I expected."

Our facts: The listing says 350 ml and 9 cm tall. Returns are accepted within 30 days. I'll add a photo next to a standard mug.

AI reply: Thank you, I'm glad you like the design. I'm sorry the mug was smaller than you expected. It holds 350 ml and is 9 cm tall, and I'm adding a photo next to a standard mug so the size is clearer. If you'd like to return it, you can within 30 days. Just send me a message.

Review (★☆☆☆☆): "Took forever to arrive."

Our facts: Shipped the day after the order. It took 12 days longer than the estimate to arrive.

AI reply: Thank you for your review, and I'm sorry your order took so long to reach you. I shipped it the day after you ordered, but it arrived 12 days later than expected. I know that's frustrating when you're waiting. If there's anything I can help with, please send me a message.

  • It used only our facts. No made-up discounts or promises.
  • It didn't blame anyone for the late order, as the prompt asked. It said what happened and stopped there.
  • It kept the offer clear: replacement or refund, return within 30 days, or a message.
  • One thing to watch: the second reply says "I'm adding a photo". Only keep that if you really will.

Check before you post

  • Every fact is true: dates, sizes, what you offer.
  • No personal details: no name, address, order or tracking number.
  • No blame on the buyer, the delivery company or the platform.
  • It sounds like you. Change words the AI chose that you would never say.
  • You're ready: on Etsy you get one reply, and the review is locked after it.

When to report a review

Etsy removes a review only if it breaks its policies. Its list includes private information, harassing or hateful language, spam, threats, and reviews only about things outside your control, like the delivery company or Etsy. If a review breaks a rule, report it instead of arguing in a reply. (How to report a review)

FAQ

Can I edit my reply to a review on Etsy?

No. Etsy says you can reply to each review only once and you can't edit the reply. You can delete it, but then you can't post another one.

Should I reply right away?

Message the buyer privately first. On Etsy, once you reply publicly, the buyer can no longer edit their review. You have 100 days from the buyer's last edit to reply.

Can Etsy remove a bad review?

Etsy says its support team can't remove a review because a seller asks. It removes reviews only if they break its policies. You can report a review that does.

Is it OK to use AI for review replies?

Yes, as a first draft. Give it only the real facts, check every sentence, and remove anything that isn't true or sounds cold.
