AI Prompts for Product Photos (With Results)

How to use these prompts

  • Start with a clear phone photo of the real product, in good light.
  • Open Gemini or ChatGPT, upload the photo and paste one prompt. Change the part in [brackets].
  • One scene per message. For the next one in the same chat, start with "Now a new photo with the same product from my first photo."
  • Want an empty cup or bowl? Add "It is empty." Without it, Gemini added a drink almost every time.

1. White background

For your main listing photo. From our white background guide.

Prompt: white background
Edit this photo: remove the background. Keep the product exactly the same: same shape, colour, details and design. Do not change or add anything on the product. Put it on a pure white background (#FFFFFF) with a soft natural shadow under the product. Product photo style.

2. Lifestyle scene

Shows the product in use. From our lifestyle photos guide.

Prompt: lifestyle scene
Use the product in this photo. Keep the product exactly the same: same shape, colour, texture and design. Do not change or add anything on the product.

Place it [describe the scene, for example: on a wooden breakfast table by a window in soft morning light, next to a plate with a croissant].

Realistic product photo, natural shadows, no text.

3. Flat lay from above

Popular for social media and Pinterest.

Prompt: flat lay from above
Use the product in this photo. Keep the product exactly the same: same shape, colour, texture and design. Do not change or add anything on the product.

Show it from directly above, as a flat lay on [a surface, for example: a light linen cloth], with [a few small props, for example: cinnamon sticks, coffee beans and a small spoon] around it.

Realistic product photo, natural shadows, no text.

What happened: the scene looks good, and the mug stayed empty because we asked. But from above, you can't see the cat design. For a product with a design on the side, use a flat lay as an extra photo, not the main one.

4. Seasonal scene

For holidays and gift season. Change the scene for spring, summer or autumn.

Prompt: seasonal scene
Use the product in this photo. Keep the product exactly the same: same shape, colour, texture and design. Do not change or add anything on the product.

Place it [describe a seasonal scene, for example: on a wooden table next to a small Christmas tree with warm fairy lights and a red knitted scarf, in cozy evening light]. Show the design facing the camera.

Realistic product photo, natural shadows, no text.

What happened: the design faced the camera, as we asked. Gemini added coffee, because we didn't say the mug was empty.

5. Colour background

A calm, simple look that matches your shop colours.

Prompt: colour background
Use the product in this photo. Keep the product exactly the same: same shape, colour, texture and design. Do not change or add anything on the product.

Place it on a plain [colour, for example: sage green] background with one soft shadow, in soft studio light. Show the design facing the camera.

Realistic product photo, no props, no text.

What happened: a clean photo, and the mug stayed empty. But our real mug is light grey, and here it looks beige. A buyer would expect a different colour, so we wouldn't use this one in a listing.

6. Pinterest photo

Leaves space at the top for your pin text. See our Pinterest pins guide for the rest.

Prompt: Pinterest photo with space for text
Use the product in this photo. Keep the product exactly the same: same shape, colour, texture and design. Do not change or add anything on the product.

Place it [describe the scene, for example: on a cream linen tablecloth in soft daylight] in the lower third of the image, with the design facing the camera. Leave the top half as calm, empty space for text. Vertical 2:3 format.

Realistic product photo, no text.

7. Mockup for a print

For printables and wall art. From our mockups guide.

Prompt: framed print mockup
Make a realistic mockup photo with this print.

Put the print [describe the frame and the scene, for example: in a thin light oak frame with a white mat, hanging on a warm white wall above a beige sofa in a bright living room].

Keep the artwork exactly the same: same text, letters, shapes, colours and position. Don't add, remove or change anything in the artwork. Portrait format.

What we learned

  • Say what you don't want. "The mug is empty" and "no props" worked when we wrote them.
  • Say which side faces the camera. "Show the design facing the camera" worked in every photo where we used it.
  • Colours drift. In most AI photos, our light grey mug came out warmer or beige. Compare every photo with the real product.
  • Fast: each photo took Gemini under 30 seconds.
  • On Etsy, keep at least one unedited photo and say in your description that you used AI. See our lifestyle guide for the rules.

FAQ

Which free AI tool is best for product photos?

In our tests, Gemini was fast and kept the design close to the original. ChatGPT made more styled scenes but changed the design more and slowed down on the free plan. Try both with your own product.

Why does the AI put a drink in my mug?

In our tests, Gemini added a drink almost every time we didn't say otherwise. When we wrote "The mug is empty", it left it empty.

Can I use these AI photos on Etsy?

Etsy asks for at least one unedited photo of the real item and a note in the description when you use AI-edited photos. Read our lifestyle photos guide for the details.

Do the prompts work for products other than mugs?

They are written for any product. We tested them on one mug and two prints, so check every result with your own product.
