How to Write Image Alt Text with AI (Free Prompt)

By Seller Notebook · Updated November 2026

In short

  • Alt text is a short description of a photo for people who can't see it. Search engines read it too.
  • Etsy allows up to 250 characters. Shopify allows 512, and recommends 125 or less.
  • Google says to describe the image and not to fill alt text with keywords.
  • AI can write it from the photo in seconds. Check it against the photo before you paste it.

What alt text is for

Alt text describes a photo in words. Screen readers read it out to people who can't see the photo. It also shows if the image can't load, and it helps search engines understand the image.

Good alt text says what is in the photo, in one plain sentence. It is not a list of keywords.

What Etsy, Shopify and Google say

Checked in November 2026:

  • Etsy: up to 250 characters per image, and you can add it to as many images as you want. Etsy says adding your shop name, title or tags won't give an extra SEO benefit. If you don't add alt text, Etsy may add an AI-written one for US sellers. (Etsy Help Center)
  • Shopify: up to 512 characters, with 125 or less recommended. The media editor may also suggest alt text for you. (Shopify Help Center)
  • Google: write useful alt text that fits the page, and avoid filling it with keywords, which can look like spam. (Google Search Central)

The prompt

Upload one photo to Claude, Gemini or ChatGPT, and paste this prompt. Fill in your product facts.

Prompt: alt text for a product photo
Write alt text for this product photo for my [Etsy / Shopify / online store] listing.

Product facts (use them only if they match the photo): [what it is, material, colour, design]

Rules:
- Describe only what you can see: the product first, then its colour and design, then the setting.
- One sentence, under 125 characters.
- Don't start with "Image of" or "Photo of".
- Don't add keywords, prices or my shop name.
- Don't guess the size or material if you can't see it and it's not in my facts.

For the next photo in the same chat, upload it and write "Now the same for this photo."

Our test: 4 photos of one mug

We ran the prompt in Claude in November 2026, on four photos of our cat mug: our real photo and three AI-edited ones. Our facts were "ceramic mug, black cat and moon design".

Tap a photo to see it bigger. The text under each photo is the alt text Claude wrote.

  • Every one was one clear sentence, under 125 characters, with no keywords stuffed in.
  • It used our facts ("ceramic") and described the rest from the photo.
  • It described the photo, not the product. Our real mug is light grey, but in two AI photos it looks beige, so the alt text says beige. That is correct alt text. It also showed us that those AI photos changed the colour of our mug.
  • It mentioned things the AI photo added, like the coffee in the mug. Fine for alt text, but a buyer gets an empty mug.

What not to write: "cat mug black cat mug cute mug gift for cat lover coffee mug ceramic mug". That is a keyword list, not a description.

Check before you paste

  • Is it true for this photo? Look at the photo while you read it.
  • Is it true for your product? If the alt text shows a colour or detail your product doesn't have, the photo may need fixing, not the text.
  • Is it one sentence, and within the limit?
  • No guesses: no size, material or brand that you didn't give.

Where to paste it

  • Etsy: Shop Manager → Listings → pick the listing → pick the photo → Alt text → Apply. In the Etsy Seller app, edit the listing and use the Alt text icon under Photo and video.
  • Shopify: Products → pick the product → click the image → Add alt text → Save alt text.

FAQ

Does alt text help Etsy SEO?

Etsy says that adding your shop name, listing title, description or tags to alt text won't give your listing an extra SEO benefit. Write it to describe the photo.

How long can alt text be?

On Etsy, up to 250 characters. On Shopify, up to 512, but Shopify recommends 125 or less.

Can AI write alt text for all my photos?

Yes, it can write a first draft for each photo. Check every one against the photo, because AI describes what the photo shows, not what your real product looks like.

What if I don't add alt text on Etsy?

Etsy says that if you don't add it, it may add an AI-written description to listing images for US sellers. Writing your own lets you check it.
