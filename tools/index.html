Tools tested

Honest notes on AI tools for online sellers. A tool is only listed here after it has been tried on real tasks.

How tools are tested

  • Each tool is used for a real seller task, such as writing a listing or cleaning up a product photo.
  • Free plans are tested first, because most small sellers start there.
  • Prices and plan limits are checked on the tool's official page on the day of writing.
  • Screenshots come from the actual test, not from the tool's marketing pages.
  • No company can pay to be listed or to get a better review.
